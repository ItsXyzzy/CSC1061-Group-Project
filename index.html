Welcome to Track & Taste

Your guide to exploring European cuisines and cultures through interrailing.

Why Interrail?

🌿

Eco-Friendly

See Europe with a lower carbon footprint. Train travel is one of the greenest ways to explore.

🗺️

Freedom

Hop on and off wherever you please. Your pass gives you the flexibility to change plans on a whim.

🏛️

Culture

Immerse yourself in local traditions. From food to history, experience the authentic heart of Europe.

Day 7-8 Berlin Experience German favorites including bratwurst, pretzels, and schnitzel.
